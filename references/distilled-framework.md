# Michael大麦哥 · operational synthesis

This is a cross-video synthesis, not a claim that the creator endorses every wording. Use the search script and original sources before quoting.

## 1. Stable self-worth beats comparison

**Pattern:** Comparison often uses another person's position to confirm one's own position. The antidote is a less externally indexed sense of worth.

**Use when:** envy, status anxiety, social comparison, or feeling behind.

**Practice:** Name what external rank is being used as evidence of worth; separate the other person's outcome from your own evaluation; choose one concrete contribution or relationship you value without needing an audience.

**Sources:** “为什么别人过得比你好，你会不舒服”; “爱自己的四个约定”; “真正的成就，不是满足别人，而是成为你自己。”

## 2. Acceptance is not surrender

**Pattern:** Accept limits and unfinished areas without abandoning agency. The distinction is between stopping self-attack and stopping all effort.

**Use when:** burnout, perfectionism, “I should be better,” or repeated self-criticism.

**Practice:** State the real constraint; remove one nonessential demand; retain one small action that is still within control.

**Sources:** “我开始放过自己”; “我们好像越来越习惯要求自己变得更好”; “自我接纳，从这20个问题开始。”

## 3. Low periods need stability before a dramatic comeback

**Pattern:** A low period is not a competition for whose pain is worse, and recovery does not have to become a heroic reinvention.

**Use when:** unemployment, illness, breakup, failed launch, or loss of direction.

**Practice:** Protect sleep, food, income runway, and one reliable routine; reduce the problem to the next maintainable action; postpone identity-level conclusions.

**Sources:** “走出低谷，不一定需要一场逆袭”; “38岁失业，但这5个问题帮我抵抗了焦虑”; “40岁四大皆‘空’，我怎么走出来的。”

## 4. Separate emotion from fact, then act

**Pattern:** Feelings are real experiences but not automatically accurate descriptions of reality. Overthinking can itself become the source of anxiety.

**Use when:** rumination, catastrophizing, mind reading, or indecision.

**Practice:** Write the feared conclusion; ask what verifiable evidence supports it; identify the smallest reversible experiment; decide what action will be taken before the next review.

**Sources:** “跟自己过不去的‘10种认知陷阱’”; “真正让你焦虑的，不是想法，而是‘思考’”; “掌控选择｜3个在不确定中做出决定的方法”.

## 5. Build agency through small commitments

**Pattern:** Identity changes through repeated evidence. Making an action small increases the chance of keeping the promise to oneself.

**Use when:** habit formation, procrastination, or low motivation.

**Practice:** Shrink the action until it is almost impossible to refuse; record completion; attach the action to an identity statement (“I am someone who trains/reads/creates”); review the process rather than only the outcome.

**Sources:** “为什么养成习惯那么难！”; “如何把人生的‘主体性’拿回来？”; “跑步不一定要带运动手表”.

## 6. Treat the body as a long-term asset

**Pattern:** Exercise is framed as regained control, future capacity, recovery capability, and evidence that one can influence one's life—not only appearance or performance.

**Use when:** exercise planning, aging, health anxiety, or rebuilding confidence.

**Practice:** Pick a sustainable mode; alternate activities; train for future independence rather than only short-term appearance; use perceived exertion when recovery is poor; avoid treating exercise as a cure for unrelated problems.

**Sources:** “关于运动，我想告诉你的4件事”; “身体｜是我们最后的秩序”; “铁人三项｜重要的5+1种营养”.

## 7. Relationships need independence, clarity, and boundaries

**Pattern:** Love is presented as something to experience, not fully solve. Recurring mechanisms include independence, space, mutual effort, communication, attraction, and resistance to outside opinion.

**Use when:** relationship conflict, over-giving, fear of speaking up, or confusion about compatibility.

**Practice:** State the need directly instead of requiring mind reading; distinguish support from self-erasure; examine whether independence and space exist on both sides; discuss the behavior rather than assigning a global identity.

**Sources:** “关于爱情，我终于想明白了几件事”; “亲密关系中我们忽略了哪4件事？”; “爱自己的四个约定”.

## 8. Freedom is responsibility plus direction

**Pattern:** Freedom is not the absence of rules or the pursuit of every impulse. It involves choosing a direction, accepting responsibility, and building enough structure to act without external instruction.

**Use when:** career transition, self-employment, too many options, or resistance to social expectations.

**Practice:** Define the constraint you are unwilling to violate; choose the next stage rather than the final life plan; give the choice a review date; take responsibility for the trade-off.

**Sources:** “自由，可能是成年人最难的一课”; “遇事不决｜可问本心”; “你的选择，就是对的。”

## 9. Time is shaped by attention and novelty

**Pattern:** Feeling that time passes too quickly is tied to automatic routines, fragmented attention, and lack of memorable experience.

**Use when:** time scarcity, phone overuse, monotony, or wanting more presence.

**Practice:** Create novelty; do one thing at a time; leave deliberate unstructured time; exercise; record ordinary moments; plan one thing worth anticipating.

**Sources:** “让时间慢下来的10个方法”; “你的快乐为什么越来越少”; “不要活在‘昨天和明天’”.

## 10. Career decisions should be evaluated as systems of incentives

**Pattern:** Career advice emphasizes agency, manager incentives, interviews, salary negotiation, transitions, and the difference between being used and being developed.

**Use when:** job change, promotion, manager conflict, or interview preparation.

**Practice:** Ask who benefits from the current arrangement; assess whether the manager is investing in your growth; negotiate explicit scope and compensation; test a transition before making it irreversible.

**Sources:** “职场篇：为什么要离职和跳槽？”; “职场篇：老板是在用你，还是在培养你？”; “职场篇：‘面试必备’-薪资怎么谈？”

## 11. Meaning is built inside finitude

**Pattern:** Mortality and impermanence are used to reorder priorities, not to justify pessimism. Ordinary days and finite relationships become more valuable when their endings are acknowledged.

**Use when:** loss, mortality, FOMO, meaninglessness, or misplaced ambition.

**Practice:** Ask what would still matter if recognition disappeared; choose what deserves attention now; state the relationship or experience you do not want to postpone; let meaning come from chosen commitments rather than an external guarantee.

**Sources:** “为什么我们应该热爱一个终将失去的世界？”; “当我们离开的时候，谁会坐在葬礼的第一排？”; “第二条命，是你意识到你只有一条的那一刻。”

## 12. Independent judgment requires provenance

**Pattern:** Repeated claims and social consensus are not substitutes for examining where an idea came from and whether it survives evidence.

**Use when:** misinformation, crowd pressure, doctrine, or “everyone says so”.

**Practice:** Identify the claim, its source, what would falsify it, and whether the speaker has relevant evidence; keep uncertainty explicit; change position when evidence changes.

**Sources:** “‘大家都这么说’，就一定是对的吗？”; “你是否质疑过你相信的一切？”; “有些曾经深信不疑的事，我现在不信了。”

## 13. Creative work compounds through observation and recording

**Pattern:** Photography and video are used as attention practices: recording makes ordinary experience more visible and serves as evidence of one's existence.

**Use when:** creative block, personal branding, journaling, or feeling unworthy of being recorded.

**Practice:** Notice light, composition, and what you want to preserve; document before optimizing; publish consistently enough to create feedback; let evidence of past work inform identity rather than prove superiority.

**Sources:** “你｜值得被记录”; “成为博主900天，它改变了我对vlog的看法”; “做自媒体带来的5个好处｜钱只是1个”.
